Mathematical Model

1. Effective reactance

X eff = X L ( 1 − k s )

Series compensation inserts a capacitive reactance in series with the transmission line. The resulting effective line reactance is smaller than the uncompensated reactance.

2. Net reactive power

Q net = Q L − Q sh

3. Receiving-end power

S = P L + j Q net

4. Transmission equation

V s ¯ = V r ¯ + ( R + j X eff ) I

5. Current

I = S ¯ V r ¯

6. Power-angle relation

P ≈ V s X eff V r sin δ

7. Approximate maximum power

P max ≈ V s 2 X eff
